# Frontend Specification

## Design objective

The site must feel like an editorial intelligence product, not an internal BI dashboard.
It should be understandable by a LinkedIn visitor on mobile.

## Home / Hero

Headline:
**Alexia no llena estadios. Los llenas tú.**

Subhead:
Where are London City Lionesses' next recurring spectators - and what should the club do to acquire them?

Hero modules:
- next home fixture
- current decision: ATTACK / TEST / DEFEND
- recommended territory
- one sentence explaining why

## Page 1 - Opportunity Map

Interactive map of South London.

Layers:
- LSOA opportunity
- girls-football nodes
- Hayes Lane
- Tier-1 competitors
- local women's clubs

Click territory -> Play Card.

## Page 2 - Territory Play Cards

Show:
- opportunity score
- family households
- family density
- girls network
- Sunday travel time
- nearest competitor
- competition pressure
- recommended acquisition play
- recommended ticket product

## Page 3 - Matchday Decision

For next fixture:
- Planning Score
- Live Score
- Territory
- Calendar Whitespace
- Attention Pressure
- Weather
- Attendance Momentum
- Fixture Appeal
- Product
- Channel
- Message
- KPI

This should be the most shareable LinkedIn screenshot.

## Page 4 - Season Calendar

Timeline/table of all home games.
Colour-coded:
- ATTACK HARD
- ATTACK
- TEST
- DEFEND CORE

Allow drill-down into why.

## Page 5 - Competition

Map + comparison:
- Crystal Palace
- Charlton
- Chelsea
- Arsenal
- Tottenham
- West Ham
- local Tier 3-5 clubs

Show division, venue, distance and territorial pressure.

## Page 6 - Ticketing Lab

Compare product ladders:
- London City
- Arsenal
- Chelsea
- Tottenham
- Palace
- Charlton
- West Ham

Separate:
- observed product
- inferred strategic objective

## Page 7 - Local Market

Show:
- Bromley footfall
- leisure expenditure growth
- restaurant/cafe growth
- sport/recreation growth
- business sectors
- local activation inventory

## Page 8 - Learning Loop

As real match data arrives:
- tickets sold
- scans
- attendance
- realised £/ticket
- first-time households
- repeat <=90d
- product utilisation
- channel attribution

## Visual style

- editorial / modern sports intelligence
- lots of whitespace
- one accent colour family
- large numeric cards
- map-led storytelling
- minimal technical jargon on public pages
- methodology available in expandable panels

## Mobile-first requirement

Every key insight must fit as a portrait screenshot for LinkedIn carousel use.
