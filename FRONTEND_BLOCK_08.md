# Frontend Block 08

Visual identity refresh inspired by London City Lionesses' 2026/27 home-kit direction.

## Official source basis
London City describes the 2026/27 home shirt as:
- Hyperturq base
- navy Nike / name / number details
- club crest recreated through lino-print artwork

The club does not publish a complete digital UI palette with exact hex values in the referenced material.

## Therefore
The CSS values in this prototype are deliberately approximate and are labelled as
London City-inspired rather than official brand specifications.

## Adds
- Hyperturq-inspired accent
- Navy background system
- White/cloud typography
- Turquoise score and signal treatments
- Updated map, story, weather and live-state surfaces
- Prototype branding disclaimer
- No external assets or club crest copied into the app
