# AVELA Commercial V2

## Positioning

**Category:** Decision intelligence for football clubs.

**Primary promise:** Read the signals. Move the club.

**Core distinction:** Specialist tools remain systems of record. AVELA becomes the decision layer across them.

### Messaging guardrails

- Do not position AVELA as a replacement for Blinkfire, CRM, ticketing, BI or work-management tools.
- Do not describe AVELA as a chatbot or an LLM wrapper.
- Do not imply that missing calendar or capacity data means available.
- Do not claim club adoption from the independent London City case.
- Do not promise automated material decisions without authorised human approval.
- Prefer cross-domain decision examples over generic AI claims.

## Primary objections to resolve

- We already have Blinkfire.
- We already have Power BI / dashboards.
- We already have ChatGPT or Claude.
- We already have Asana / Monday / Teams.
- This sounds like a large integration project.
- AI should not make club decisions automatically.
- We do not have perfectly clean or connected data.
- How does AVELA deal with player availability, contracts, calendars and internal information?
- What would a low-risk pilot actually look like?

## Lead strategy

Primary CTA: **See AVELA on a real fixture**

Secondary CTA: **Discuss a club pilot**

Final CTA: **See how AVELA could fit your club**

Avoid high-friction forms on first contact. A future lead form should initially request only:
- Club
- Role
- Work email
- Optional biggest challenge

Suggested challenge choices:
- Matchday growth
- Sponsorship
- Player assets
- Campaign coordination
- Operational capacity
- Other
